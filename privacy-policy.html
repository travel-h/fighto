7. Your choices and rights

  • Stop all collection: uninstall the App.
  • Access or deletion: because we do not ask for your name or email, we usually cannot link data to you. You can still contact us at [rutow4770@gmail.com] and we will help, including with requests under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA.
